Phase 04

The Case For Escaping

The “why” behind all of this. Why it matters so much. Why people should want to get on board.

“The algorithm doesn’t show you what matters. It shows you what keeps you staring. There’s a billion-dollar difference between the two.”
01
Your Attention Is Being Sold
Every second you spend in a feed is inventory on an ad exchange. You are not the customer. You are the product being auctioned.
02
Your Choices Aren't Yours
Recommendation engines don't serve you options. They serve you outcomes—ones that benefit the platform, not you.
03
Your Mental Health Is At Stake
Anxiety, comparison, outrage, loneliness—these aren't side effects. They're engagement features. By design.
04
Democracy Needs You Present
A society of algorithmically-isolated individuals can't self-govern. Shared reality requires shared spaces—not personalized bubbles.
05
Creativity Demands Freedom
You can't create something original when your entire input stream is optimized for sameness and conformity.
06
Your Kids Are Watching
The next generation will inherit whatever relationship with technology we normalize today. Make it one worth inheriting.
